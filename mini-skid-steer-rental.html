Mini skid steer rental FAQ

How much does it cost to rent the Bobcat MT100?

Standard posted rates are $225 daily for up to 8 machine hours, $225 weekend for up to 8 machine hours or $299 weekend for up to 16 machine hours, and $795 weekly for up to 7 consecutive days / 40 machine hours. Multi-day pricing is $175/day for 3–4 days. Attachments, delivery, applicable sales tax, fuel, cleaning, and other terms are confirmed before booking.

Do you deliver the mini skid steer?

Yes. Local delivery and pickup are available based on location, scheduling, and current equipment availability. Pickup may also be available for customers with the proper towing setup.

What attachments are available?

The bucket is included with the MT100 rental. Pallet forks and auger options are available for material handling, fence posts, deck footers, sign posts, and similar projects.

Do you rent to homeowners?

Yes. Pinnacle Equipment serves contractors, property owners, homeowners, landscapers, and customers working on practical property projects.

Do you offer contractor pricing?

Yes. Contractor pricing and account setup are available for approved commercial customers. Standard public pricing is shown on the rental page and estimator; contractor pricing is confirmed separately.
