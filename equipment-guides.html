Pinnacle Equipment Trailer Quick Reference

MT100 Loading & Tie-Down Guide

Use this page as a quick reference when transporting the Bobcat MT100 with the Pinnacle Equipment trailer. Follow the loading position and tie-down examples shown below before entering the roadway.
Before You Move
Four-Point Securement
Pinnacle's standard setup is intentionally simple and repeatable.
  • 4 individual rated chains
  • 4 individual rated binders
  • 4 designated MT100 tie-down points
  • Machine positioned at the marked transport point
If anything does not match the photos on this page, stop and contact Pinnacle Equipment before transporting the machine.
Loading

Load it the same way every time

Follow the steps below and compare your setup with the photos of the Pinnacle trailer and MT100. Tap any photo to enlarge the details.
01
Check the trailer first
Before loading, verify the trailer is completely connected and ready to accept the machine.
  • Coupler locked
  • Safety chains connected
  • Breakaway cable connected
  • Electrical plug connected
  • Jack fully raised
  • Tilt lock engaged for transport
02
Back the MT100 on
With the standard bucket, align the machine straight with the trailer and back onto the deck slowly. Keep the bucket low and keep bystanders clear.
03
Stop at the marked position
Continue backward until the designated reference point on the MT100 lines up with the marked transport position on the trailer.
04
Lower and shut down
Center the machine, lower the bucket or approved attachment completely, return the controls to neutral, and shut the engine off before installing the tie-downs.

Final Walk-Around

Check it before you pull away

Walk completely around the trailer and verify each item below before entering the roadway.
Machine is centered and aligned with the marked transport position
Bucket or approved attachment is fully lowered
Engine is off
Front-left chain and binder are secure
Front-right chain and binder are secure
Rear-left chain and binder are secure
Rear-right chain and binder are secure
No chains or loose items are dragging or hanging
Coupler, safety chains, breakaway cable and electrical plug are connected
Trailer jack is raised, tilt lock is engaged, and lights are working

Attachments

Attachment-specific transport

Compare the attachment on your rental with the relevant example below. Confirm the transport setup with Pinnacle Equipment at pickup.
Bucket / Forks
Bucket/Fork combo are typically transported on machine or on stationary section of the trailer if the auger is also being transported. Forks alone may be done in a similar fashion.
Using forks? Confirm the fork transport position and securement with Pinnacle Equipment at pickup.
Auger
The auger is typically transported attached to the machine. Compare the bit position and securement with the setup shown below.
Need Help?
